Taking Care of Your Vinyl Collection

I've been into records lately because of the crisp sound they produce vs Bluetooth radio. Since the weather is cold, I tend to gravitate to grungy artists like Flyleaf, PinkNoise, Slipknot, Rob Zombie, Deftones, Evenessence.

Essential Vinyl Care Tips

  • Keep Records Clean: Use a soft brush or microfiber cloth before each play.
  • Store Vertically: Avoid stacking records to prevent warping.
  • Control Humidity: Keep your collection in a dry, stable environment.
  • Handle Properly: Always hold records by the edges to avoid fingerprints.
  • Use a Quality Turntable: Ensure your needle is in good condition and set to the right weight.
  • Rotate Playing Time: Don’t play the same record continuously to preserve grooves.
