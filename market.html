Facebook Marketplace: Safe & Stylish

Just like thrifting, Facebook Marketplace is a great place to get second-hand clothes and furniture. I love discovering unique items while keeping safety top of mind.

Staying Safe During Exchanges

  • Meet in well-lit, public places (preferably police stations or busy parking lots).
  • Bring a friend along for added safety.
  • Inspect items carefully before purchasing.
  • Avoid sharing personal information beyond what’s necessary for the transaction.
  • Use cash or secure payment methods.
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels off, walk away.

My Furniture Style: Modern Boho & Mid-Century Maximalism

I source furniture that bridges the gap between Mid-Century structure and Bohemian soul. My focus is on finding 'storied' pieces—like walnut credenzas with iconic tapered legs—that serve as a timeless foundation for a lush, maximalist home. Think organic materials meets geometric precision, curated for a space that feels both sophisticated and lived-in.

Core Styles

  • Modern Boho: Clean-lined furniture as a quiet base for textured, "loud" accessories.
  • Mid-Century Maximalism: “More is more” philosophy grounded with 1950s-60s wood furniture.
  • Dark & Moody Academia: Deep tones, library-style gallery walls, and a collected-over-time feel.

Descriptive Adjectives

  • Sourced & Storied: Vintage, secondhand, or pieces with history.
  • Organic & Structured: Natural woods/plants combined with geometric lines.
  • Cozy & Curated: Intentionally full of items that bring joy.

Key Furniture Characteristics

  • Silhouettes: Tapered “peg” legs, splayed feet, low-profile horizontal forms.
  • Materials: Warm hardwoods (teak, walnut, oak), rattan, wicker, leather.
  • Functionality: Form follows function — practical yet sculptural.
